01Overview

Talib Alillm helps people learn Islamic subjects through structured, teacher-led courses. The platform brings three groups into one experience: students who learn, teachers who deliver, and administrators who keep everything running.

I designed the product end to end, from understanding the users and structuring the content to the final interfaces on web and mobile.

UsersStudents, teachers, administrators
ScopeEnd-to-end, web and mobile
FocusStructured, intuitive learning
02The Challenge

Learning is scattered. The path isn’t clear.

Islamic learning online often lives across separate channels and tools, with no clear path from a first lesson to deeper study. Learners struggle to know where to start, which teacher to trust, and how far they have come. Teachers and administrators, meanwhile, manage courses and students with too much manual effort.

How might wemake structured Islamic learning feel clear, trustworthy, and easy to follow for every kind of user?
03Goals

What the design had to achieve.

01

Structure the journey

Give learners a clear path through courses, levels, and progress.

02

Serve three roles

Design focused experiences for students, teachers, and administrators inside one system.

03

Build trust

Make teachers, course content, and ratings easy to understand so learners choose with confidence.

04

Stay consistent everywhere

Keep one design language that works naturally on both web and mobile.

04Users

Three roles, one platform.

Learner

Student

Wants to learn at their own pace and see their progress.

Needs

  • Find the right course quickly
  • Follow lessons step by step
  • Track study hours and achievements

Pain points

  • Unclear where to start
  • No sense of progress
Educator

Teacher

Wants to deliver courses and stay close to their students.

Needs

  • Present their profile and courses clearly
  • Manage students and lessons in one place

Pain points

  • Manual coordination
  • Little visibility into student progress
Operator

Administrator

Keeps the platform organized, accurate, and running.

Needs

  • Oversee users, courses, and activity
  • Make updates without friction

Pain points

  • Scattered tools
  • Hard to get a clear overview
05My Approach

From questions to interfaces.

A clear, repeatable process: understand first, structure second, and design last.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Output: research notes, goals

    Understand the learners, the teachers, and how the content is organized today.

  2. 02

    Define

    Output: personas, scope

    Turn what I learned into clear problems, priorities, and a focused scope.

  3. 03

    Structure

    Output: sitemap, user flows

    Organize the content and map the paths each role takes to get things done.

  4. 04

    Design

    Output: wireframes, UI, design system

    Start with low-fidelity layouts, then build the visual language and the final screens.

  5. 05

    Refine

    Output: prototype, improvements

    Review the flows with real scenarios and improve what feels unclear.
